Support

How do maps get onto the watch? On the iPhone, open Offline maps, tap the areas you want and choose "Sync with watch". The watch downloads the maps itself over Wi-Fi or via the iPhone. Keep TrainMap open on the watch if it takes a while.

How do I follow a route? Import a GPX or FIT course file under Routes on the iPhone and send it to the watch. If you leave the route, the watch alerts you (adjustable under Settings › Map and route).

Where do I start an interval workout? On the watch, choose "Workout" in the sport list, tap a plan, then Play. You create your own plans on the iPhone under Intervals.

How do I pause a workout? Press the Digital Crown and the side button together. On Apple Watch Ultra, the Action button plus the side button works too.

The map shows no track or "Precise location off". On the watch, turn on "Precise Location" under Settings › Privacy › Location Services › TrainMap.

Treadmill distance is off. After the run, tap "Treadmill" on the summary and enter the treadmill's distance. The watch learns a factor for your next runs.

Restore a purchase. On the iPhone, open the Pro page and tap "Restore purchases".

Note: TrainMap is not a medical device. Heart rate, power and distance values are sensor estimates and do not replace medical advice.
