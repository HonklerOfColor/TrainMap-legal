TrainMap – Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

TrainMap does not collect any data for the developer. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and in Apple Health.

Apple Health

  • Read: heart rate, distance (walking/running, cycling), active energy, steps, cycling power, cadence, running power, stride length, ground contact time, functional threshold power (FTP), body weight, and your workouts and workout routes.
  • Written: your workouts with route and metrics and, if you change it in the app, your functional threshold power (FTP).
  • This data is used only inside the app (data pages, zones, analysis, history). It is not sent to the developer or third parties and is not used for advertising.

Location and motion

  • The Apple Watch uses your location only while you use the app, for the map, track, distance and route guidance. The iPhone does not ask for your location.
  • Motion & Fitness (the watch's pedometer and altimeter) is used for treadmill distance, cadence and ascent.
  • Location and motion data do not leave your device, except as part of the workout in Apple Health.

Map downloads

The app downloads offline maps over HTTPS from maps.crimemap.eu (Cloudflare R2). No personal data is sent, only the request for the map file. As with any request on the internet, the server technically sees your IP address. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the requested file (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

iCloud and transfer between iPhone and watch

  • Settings, routes, interval plans and maps go directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch (WatchConnectivity).
  • The treadmill calibration factor (one number with a date) is synced through your iCloud key-value store. Only Apple has access to it, not the developer.
  • If you export a workout as a GPX or FIT file, you decide where to share it.

Purchase

The in-app purchase "TrainMap Pro" is handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment or account data.

Deletion

Removing the app from your iPhone and watch deletes all of its data. You manage workouts in Apple Health in the Health app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at). As the developer stores no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to disclose.
